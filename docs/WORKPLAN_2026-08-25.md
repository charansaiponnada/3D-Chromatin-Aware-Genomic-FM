## 2. Where the project actually stands

Read `CLAUDE.md` §4 in full. The one-paragraph version:

The loss comparison is null (+0.0020 bits, wrong sign, inside noise). Two
claims about *why* were retracted on 2026-08-17. The measured reason is
**keep(φ) = 0.0573** — only 5.7% of φ's variance lies within a 32,768 bp
window, and the mechanism biases Δ per position, so that 5.7% is its entire
budget. There **is** one clean positive: probe B on within-window insulation,
where every structural seed beats every baseline seed (structural
+0.0254/+0.0303/+0.0255 vs baseline +0.0134/+0.0146/+0.0221).

**The two things blocking a publishable positive are both fixable without a
GPU:**

- **Power.** At 3 vs 3 the exact two-sided permutation test has 20
  arrangements, so the minimum attainable p is 2/20 = **0.100**. p < 0.05 is
  unreachable by design. Every GPU-hour at n=3 buys an experiment that has
  pre-committed to non-significance.
- **Signal.** keep(φ) = 0.0573. Widening the window to 65,536 raises it to
  0.1099 but leaves **89.0% of the variance still between windows**. Widening
  is a partial mitigation, not a fix.

### T1 — Raise φ resolution from 5 kb to 1 kb (or 2 kb)

**No GPU. Highest-leverage change available. Do this first.**

**Why.** keep(φ) is low partly because φ is a step function on 5 kb bins and a
window spans only ~6.6 (at 32 kb) or ~13.1 (at 65 kb) of them. At 1 kb a
65,536 bp window spans ~65 bins. This raises within-window structural variance
**without touching window size, memory, or step time** — the two levers are
independent, and only one of them has been tried.

`resolution_bp: 5000` is a config choice, not a data limit:
`data/pilot_manifest.json` records the source as a multi-resolution `.mcool`
(`4DNFIXP4QG5B`), and `phase1_acquire.py:159` opens it as
`cooler.Cooler(h5[f"resolutions/{RESOLUTION}"])`.

#### T1.1 — Confirm which resolutions exist (seconds, range reads only)

```bash
./3d-gen/bin/python -u -c "
import fsspec, h5py
URL = 'https://4dn-open-data-public.s3.amazonaws.com/fourfront-webprod/wfoutput/d6abea45-b0bb-4154-9854-1d3075b98097/4DNFIXP4QG5B.mcool'
fh = fsspec.open(URL, mode='rb', block_size=4<<20).open()
h5 = h5py.File(fh, 'r')
print(sorted(int(k) for k in h5['resolutions'].keys()))
"
```

**STOP GATE.** If 1000 is not in the list, use the smallest available above
1000 and record which. Do not proceed on an assumption about what the file
contains.

#### T1.2 — Cost check before committing to a full fetch

At 5 kb the band is **7,108,483 entries / 27,857,424 bytes** for **27,679
bins** (`data/pilot_manifest.json`, measured). Band width is
`band_bins = BAND_BP // RESOLUTION` = 2,000,000 // 5,000 = 400 bins.

At 1 kb: 138,395 bins and a 2,000-bin band. Entries scale roughly as
`n_bins × band_bins`, i.e. (5000/1000)² = **25×**.
**Projection, not a measurement: 7,108,483 × 25 ≈ 178M entries.** T1.3 measures
the real number; do not quote the projection anywhere else.

Two real risks at 1 kb, both of which must be measured rather than assumed:

- **Sparsity and balancing.** Balanced (ICE) weights get unreliable for
  low-coverage bins at fine resolution. `MIN_STRUCT_FRAC = 0.50`
  (`phase1_dataset.py:54`) may start dropping a large fraction of windows. At
  5 kb, 21,519 of 27,679 bins were usable (77.7%). **Measure the equivalent
  fraction at 1 kb.**
- **Fetch time.** The `.mcool` is read over HTTP range requests and is never
  downloaded. 25× the entries means substantially more range reads, and the
  culler will kill a long fetch. Cache per tile so a kill costs one tile.

#### T1.3 — Staged measurement on a subset

Do **not** rebuild the whole chromosome first. Fetch a ~10 Mb slice of chr9 at
1 kb and at 2 kb (`TILE_BP` is already 10,000,000, so one tile is the natural
unit), build φ on it, and report:

1. usable-bin fraction at each resolution, against 77.7% at 5 kb;
2. `keep(φ)` at 32,768 and 65,536 on that slice, against **0.0490 / 0.1099**
   measured at 5 kb (`results/novel_model/p5_window_scan.json`);
3. wall time and bytes for the slice, so the full-chromosome cost is a
   measured extrapolation rather than a guess.

**STOP GATE — report these three numbers to the PI before fetching the whole
chromosome.** The decision rule agreed in advance: if keep(φ) at 65,536 does
not at least **double** versus the 5 kb value (0.1099) while keeping the usable
fraction above ~60%, 1 kb is not worth the fetch — fall back to 2 kb and repeat,
or stop and report that resolution is not the lever either. That would itself be
a publishable measurement: it would show the constraint is intrinsic to Hi-C's
spatial autocorrelation and not an artefact of binning.

### T2 — Re-power the result you already have

**No GPU. Uses checkpoints that already exist. Can run today.**

The seed does not have to be the unit of analysis. `p5_positional_probe.json`
was computed over **274 val windows** with positions every 512 bp (17,536 val
rows). Currently the arm difference is tested across 3 seeds, which caps p at
0.100. Instead:

1. Compute the structural − baseline probe-B difference **per window**.
2. Test across windows with a **block bootstrap**. Blocks must be sized to
   Hi-C autocorrelation, which runs over **megabases** — i.i.d. resampling of
   windows is invalid here and will produce a confidently wrong p-value. This
   is the same autocorrelation that makes known weakness 5 (within-chr9 splits)
   a real problem; do not let it in through the back door.
3. Report **both**: consistent in 3/3 seeds (seed-level permutation, p = 0.100,
   the attainable floor), and the within-seed across-position result. They
   answer different questions — "does this reproduce across training runs" and
   "does this hold across the genome" — and a reviewer will want both.

**Note.** `scripts/phase5_*.py` glob `NOVEL.glob("structural_seed*")` and
`BASE.glob("baseline_v2_seed*")` at the top level
(`phase5_positional_probe.py:226–228`). The 32 kb checkpoints were moved to
`results/{novel_model,baselines}/w32768/`, so these globs now find **nothing**.
Point them at `w32768/` before re-running anything from P5.

**Acceptance:** a p-value with its resampling scheme stated, the block size
stated and justified, and both tests reported side by side.

### T5 — Design changes. **PI GATE — do not implement without sign-off.**

All three are `architecture_spec.md` §7 / §4.1.3 changes. Write the proposal,
get the decision, then implement. They are listed in descending order of
expected effect on getting a genuine positive.

#### T5a — Auxiliary structural objective

`W_dstruct` is zero-initialised (`model.py:152`), which gives the clean property
that the structural model is numerically identical to the baseline at step 0.
It also means the pathway must *discover* its own usefulness from a weak
gradient in 2,000 steps, against an input whose within-window variance is 5.7%.
D1 measured it inert in all three seeds.

Both comparable models that report positive results force the issue rather than
hoping: **EpiGePT** adds a cosine penalty tying attention to observed HiChIP
loops; **EpiBERT** uses a masked-accessibility pretraining objective. Proposal:
a small auxiliary head predicting φ_t from h_t, low loss weight, **keeping the
zero-init** so the step-0 identity property survives. This directly targets the
D1-inert finding and is the most evidence-backed change available.

Caveat to state in the proposal: an auxiliary φ-prediction loss makes the
downstream φ probes partly circular. The probe protocol withholds φ at probe
time (S0 zeros), which helps, but the honest framing changes from "the model
spontaneously learned structure" to "the model was asked to retain structure and
did." Both are reportable; they are not the same claim.

#### T5b — The permeability gate init (failure mode F2)

`model.py:153–160`: `w_gate.weight` is zero-init and `w_gate.bias = -4.0`. The
comment records this as a **deliberate tradeoff**, not an oversight: the
gradient through softplus is sigmoid(b_g), so b_g = −8 gives p = 3.4e-4 but
attenuates learning ~3000×, while b_g = −4 keeps p negligible (0.018) with 53×
more gradient.

**That tradeoff was measured and it lost.** D2: p_t mean 0.017234 / 0.017193 /
0.017108 over 287,309,824 evaluations, full range [0.0133, 0.0197], all mass in
one bin. 53× more gradient than −8 was still not enough to move it. Half the
mechanism has never actually run.

The tradeoff is real and cannot be waved away: raising b_g toward 0 makes p
non-negligible at init and **gives up the step-0 identity with the baseline**,
which is one of this project's cleanest guarantees. Options to put to the PI,
with the cost stated:

| b_g | p at init | gradient scale | identity at step 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| −8 | 3.4e-4 | sigmoid(−8) | preserved |
| **−4 (current)** | **0.018** | sigmoid(−4) | preserved; **measured not to move** |
| −2 | softplus(−2) | sigmoid(−2) | weakened |
| 0 | softplus(0) | sigmoid(0) | lost |

Compute the exact values with a command rather than copying this table's
symbolic entries into any report.

#### T5c — Per-window conditioning arm

At every window width that fits on this hardware, ~90% of φ's variance is
between windows (89.0% at 65,536; even 50.7% at 524,288). A **per-position** Δ
bias addresses the smaller share by construction. A complementary arm — one
structural embedding of the window's φ, injected once per window — is the only
design in the space with access to the other 90%.

Beyond being the likeliest source of a positive, this changes what the paper
*is*: from "our arm vs baseline (null)" to **"where should structure enter a
genomic LM?"** — a comparison across per-position Δ bias, per-window embedding,
attention regularisation (EpiGePT), masked-accessibility objectives (EpiBERT),
and post-hoc graph attention (CHROME). That framing survives a null on any
single arm. The current framing does not.

### T6 — The training run

Only after T1–T4 land and T5 is decided. Requires the GPU blocker resolved.

**Seeds — this is the part that determines whether a positive is even
expressible.** Minimum designs that can reach p < 0.05:

| design | arrangements | min two-sided p |
|---|---|---|
| 3v3 unpaired (what was run) | 20 | 0.100 |
| 3 paired, sign-flip | 8 | 0.250 — **worse** |
| 5 paired | 32 | 0.063 |
| **6 paired** | 64 | **0.031** ✓ |
| **5v5 unpaired** | 252 | **0.008** ✓ |

Note the trap: pairing cuts variance but *shrinks* the permutation space, so at
n=3 the paired test is worse than the unpaired one already run. **6 paired
seeds** is the minimum that both reduces variance and permits significance —
12 runs, roughly double the 80 GPU-h currently planned. If that budget is not
available, T2 is the route to significance and it costs nothing.

Paired inits are already implemented and verified (275/275 tensors bitwise
identical, `W_dstruct` still exactly zero) — but **every result currently in the
record predates that fix**, so only a re-run collects the power it buys.

**Change the primary endpoint.** Val bits/nt on masked nucleotide prediction is
dominated by local k-mer statistics; there is no strong prior reason 3D
conditioning moves it, and the benchmark literature reports pretraining loss
tracks downstream utility poorly. Pre-register the probe metrics as primary and
report loss as a secondary "no cost to sequence modelling" result. **Do this
before the runs, in `architecture_spec.md`, not after seeing the numbers** —
that is the whole point of pre-registration and it is the project's main asset.

**Add a strong baseline arm, not a weak one.** A tuned one-hot/GC model.
Probe B already shows both current arms below the GC composition floor
(+0.0802). Discover that yourself rather than from a reviewer. Under no
circumstances weaken the existing baseline to produce a separation — it is
matched-parameter, matched-compute, and now paired-init, and that is exactly
what makes any positive worth reporting.

## 6. What not to do

- Do not weaken the baseline to produce a separation.
- Do not pool the 32,768 and 65,536 indices, or the 5 kb and 1 kb φ builds.
  Different datasets, same reason v1 and v2 baselines must not be pooled.
- Do not run the `d_struct=8` no-encoder ablation — withdrawn 2026-08-17, the
  encoder was measured to *increase* within-window variance by 1.92×.
- Do not repeat "the pathway is live only in layers 12–15" or "F1 confirmed by
  measurement". Both retracted 2026-08-17.
- Do not describe the window as the fault that widening fixes — 89% of φ's
  variance is still between windows at 65,536.
- Do not launch P2 (S1/S3 pretraining) — P1 already answered that question.
- Do not edit a running `.sh`. Bash reads shell scripts incrementally by byte

- Do not read τ for the structural arm from `dt_proj` alone — `W_dstruct` is
  added before the softplus (`model.py:199`), and the resulting number looks
  entirely plausible while being the baseline's.
- Do not report a number from a run that has not reached `status: COMPLETED`.

## 7. The claim that is actually winnable

Worth keeping in view while running the above, because it determines which
numbers matter:

> Conditioning SSM timescales on chromatin structure during pretraining
> produces representations measurably more sensitive to within-window chromatin
> organisation than a matched sequence-only baseline, at no cost to sequence
> modelling — and we quantify where in the design space that conditioning must
> enter to have any effect at all.

That is a positive claim, it is supportable by probe B plus the fixes above,
and it turns keep(φ) from an excuse into a **contribution**: the quantitative
account of why per-position structural conditioning is starved at every window
width that fits on a GPU. Nobody has published that curve.

The loss null may well persist through all of the above. That is an acceptable
outcome and not a failure — it is reportable alongside the probe result, and
the honest version of this project is stronger than a fragile positive.
