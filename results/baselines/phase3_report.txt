5. DOES TRAINED tau APPROACH TAD SCALE?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  single-layer tau_max        46,319.5 tokens
  one 5 kb Hi-C bin           5,000 tokens   -> ratio 9.264
  100 kb (low end of TAD)     100,000 tokens   -> ratio 0.4632
  385 kb (the validated TAD)  385,000 tokens   -> ratio 0.1203
  relayed 16-layer heuristic  77,654.8 tokens   -> 0.7765 of 100 kb

  single-layer tau_max reaches one Hi-C bin (5 kb)?   True
  single-layer tau_max reaches TAD scale (100 kb)?    False
  relayed heuristic reaches TAD scale (100 kb)?       False
  mass at TAD scale >= 1e-04?                    False (exact fraction 6.954e-08)

  The mass test uses the EXACT per-layer fractions, not the subsampled
  summary field. The subsample reports 0.0 whenever the true value is
  below 5.0e-05, which is the regime this model is in, so reading the
  gate from it would answer 'no mass at TAD scale' by construction
  rather than by measurement.

  The 1e-04 floor was fixed by the PI on 2026-08-12, BEFORE seeds 1
  and 2 completed and before any verdict was computed, replacing an
  'any nonzero mass' test that seed 1 satisfied at 1.19e-07. See
  architecture_spec.md 4.1.4. It is not a post-hoc adjustment.

  VERDICT:
    Trained tau does NOT approach TAD scale.

    Per the decision table in architecture_spec.md 4.1.4 F4, the action
    is: re-scope to sub-TAD structural signal, or raise d_model/d_state,
    BEFORE spending Phase 4 compute -- not after.

    Read this carefully, because it is a statement about the BACKBONE,
    not about the structural mechanism. The limit applies identically to
    the baseline and to the structural arm: a model that cannot carry
    information 100 kb cannot be helped by being told where the TAD
    boundaries at that scale are. It does not say structure is useless
    -- phi also carries sub-TAD signal (insulation at 100 kb windows is
    a per-bin quantity, and the interpolated s_t varies at every
    position) -- it says the TAD-scale framing of the claim is not
    supportable at this model size.
