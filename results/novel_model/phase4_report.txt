6. WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
==============================================================================
  * Nothing about RELIANCE. This report measures benefit only. Whether the
    model uses phi at all is answered by scripts/phase4_p1_swap.py, and
    the two must not be conflated -- a flat delta is consistent with both
    an inert mechanism and a live one MLM cannot express.
  * The CHROME-style post-hoc and Evo2HiC-style distillation arms were
    never built, so the central 'structure earlier beats structure later'
    contrast is argued, not measured.
  * Splits are within chr9. Hi-C features autocorrelate over megabases, so
    held-out phi leaks more than held-out sequence -- and that bias runs
    TOWARD the structural arm.
  * d_struct = 2 with an encoder that architecture_spec 4.2bis measured
    training ~5 orders of magnitude slower than W_dstruct. The spec's own
    recommendation (drop the encoder, d_struct = 8) was not applied to
    these runs.
  * One chromosome, one cell line, one species, ~3 epochs.
