dependence in the per-window statistic (this is what sets block size)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ACF lags 1-8: +0.171 -0.051 -0.047 -0.084 -0.048 -0.037 +0.086 +0.171
  band = 2/sqrt(n) = 0.1208
  lags exceeding the band: [1, 8, 13]  (3/60 lags)
  FIRST crossing below the band is lag 2, but that is the first, not the last:
  the ACF does NOT decay cleanly -- roughly the number of exceedances noise alone would give.
  block length used: 31 windows = 1.016 Mb (max of measured lag 2 and a 1 Mb Hi-C floor 31)

  WHY THE 1 Mb FLOOR OVERRIDES THE MEASUREMENT. The measured ACF
  says dependence dies within ~0.066 Mb. That is not evidence of
  independence: d_w has sd 0.081 against a mean of 0.008, so it is
  mostly noise, and the autocorrelation of a mostly-noise statistic
  is near zero whatever the underlying data do. Hi-C dependence is a
  megabase-scale property of the DATA. Taking the short measured lag
  at face value would be letting the noise floor pick the block
  size, which is the anticonservative direction.

  READ THIS COLUMN BEFORE THE p COLUMN. The 95% CI gets NARROWER as
  the block gets longer, which is backwards -- longer blocks retain
  more dependence and must cost precision, never buy it. It is the
  signature of a bootstrap run on too few blocks: at L = 31 the
  resample is drawn from 9 blocks and at L = 61 from 5, so the
  resampling distribution is built from a handful of draws and its
  percentiles are not trustworthy. The small p-values there are an
  artefact of that, not evidence. Blocks long enough for Hi-C
  autocorrelation and enough of them for a valid bootstrap cannot
  both be had from 8.98 Mb of validation genome.

==========================================================================
WHAT THIS SUPPORTS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
==========================================================================
  The arm difference is positive in all three seeds and the pooled
  estimate is +0.01032. Neither test licenses p < 0.05:

   - across seeds, p = 0.100 and CANNOT go lower:
     3v3 has 20 arrangements, floor 0.100.
   - across windows, the two ends of the bracket disagree and the
     honest reading is between them:
       longest block with >= 20 blocks: L = 8 (0.262 Mb, 35 blocks), p = 0.0232
       -- but 0.262 Mb is SHORTER than Hi-C
          autocorrelation, so that p is anticonservative.
       at >= 1 Mb blocks, which Hi-C requires, only 5-9 blocks exist
       and the bootstrap percentiles are not valid at all.

  The binding constraint is the AMOUNT OF INDEPENDENT GENOME, not
  the choice of test. 274 contiguous val windows span 8.98 Mb; at
  megabase dependence that is single-digit effective samples. No
  resampling scheme creates information the data do not contain.
  The fix is more genome (T4, multi-chromosome), not a cleverer
  statistic -- and T4 is also what known weakness 5 requires.
